How weaving works

Two yarn systems cross at right angles: warp runs vertically, weft horizontally. Where the warp is raised, the draft cell is dark and the warp shows on the cloth. Try the 2/2 twill and follow the diagonal in the preview.

How knitting works

Knitted fabric is built from interlocking loops. Knit stitches face the front as V shapes; purls sit behind them. A tuck stitch holds its loop instead of knitting it, so the yarn piles into a raised ridge. Alternate knit and purl columns for rib, or rows for garter.
